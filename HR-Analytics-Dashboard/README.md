# HR Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview
This project analyzes employee data to understand workforce trends, employee attrition, and key HR metrics. The dashboard helps HR teams make data-driven decisions to improve employee retention and workforce management.

## Objective
- Analyze employee attrition.
- Monitor workforce demographics.
- Identify factors affecting employee turnover.
- Provide insights for HR decision-making.

## Dataset
HR Analytics Dataset (Kaggle)

## Tools Used
- Power BI
- Excel

## Key Metrics
- Total Employees
- Active Employees
- Attrition Count
- Attrition Rate (%)
- Average Age
- Average Monthly Income
- Average Years at Company

## Dashboard Visualizations
- KPI Cards
- Attrition by Department
- Attrition by Gender
- Attrition by Age Group
- Attrition by Job Role
- Attrition by Education Field
- Attrition by Marital Status
- Attrition by Salary Slab

## Key Insights
- The overall employee attrition rate provides an overview of workforce turnover.
- Some departments experience higher attrition than others.
- Employees with lower salaries tend to have higher attrition.
- Certain age groups and job roles show higher employee turnover.
- These insights can help HR teams improve retention strategies.

## Business Recommendations
- Improve employee engagement programs.
- Review compensation for high-attrition groups.
- Strengthen career development opportunities.
- Conduct regular employee satisfaction surveys.
